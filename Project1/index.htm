Web Site Critiques

Questions

  1. What was your first impression of this site?
  2. How does this site organize the flow of information? (Do they have links on the left and text on the right? Some other scheme? No scheme?)
  3. Does the site structure make sense or does it confuse you? Why?
  4. Does the site graphics/layout/multimedia enhance the site or detract from it? Why?
  5. How long did it take for the home page to load? Was the load time reasonable?
  6. Was the text color and size readable? If a background color or image was used, did it enhance or detract from the site?
  7. Did the site have a navigation bar or other navigation tool? Was the navigation well done?
  8. Did the site have a color scheme or look and feel that was repeated on all the pages?
  9. How does the site respond to smaller window sizes (responsive design)?
  10. What does this site offer that was unusual or very well done?

Trader Joes (http://www.traderjoes.com)

Answers

  1. My first impression of the site was that it reminds me of fall with the colors and products shown. Very aesthetically pleasing to the eye.
  2. The information is organized in a top to bottom format. There are links to important info such as store locations and careers. On the top left there is a menu icon that gives a more detailed directory of the site.
  3. For me the structure makes complete sense. It give a good general overview of what sorts of things you can find on the site, then it gives easy to find links to all that information. It visually divides categories into distinct sections.
  4. The site grpahics and multimedia significantly ehance the site. There are high quality pictures of food which helps to visualize what they offer. The hand drawn images help to give the site a unique feel and identity.
  5. The home page took less than two seconds to load which is very reasonable.
  6. I had no issues with reading the text. The white or lighter toned backgrounds combined with darker text was easy on the eyes. Colors used ehanced the site by contributing to the fall theme.
  7. The site did have navigation tools such as links at the top and a hamburger menu with detailed links to different parts of the site. I had no problems getting around the site and finding whatever I was looking for.
  8. Most pages had distinct fall vibes through the colors, illustrations and dexcriptions.
  9. Images and animations adjusted in size automatically when I resized the window. The design seemed very responsive and well done.
  10. One major difference between this site and the sites of other grocery stores that I noticed was the inclusion of recipes you can make at home. I thought that was a nice touch and a good way to motivate a visitor to purchase their product.
